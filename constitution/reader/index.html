The Reader displays the Zayn Constitution, the law of user interfaces.

The Reader renders the Zayn Constitution from the one data file that every conforming linter loads as law, so that a person can search, read and cite the rules that protect attention, reasoning and trust in business interfaces. The Reader ensures that the text a person reads is the same law that the tools enforce, because both load the same file. loading…

The rules of the law that the Reader itself obeys

  • Calm Over Power — nothing moves unless the person moves it, and the page makes no external requests, fetches no fonts and runs no trackers. The page works offline once it is loaded.
  • Keyboard Sovereignty (Rule 80) — everything on the page is reachable without a mouse.
  • Visible State (Rule 82) — search matches, active modes and enforcement status are always shown, and never implied.
  • Zero Displacement (Rule 38) — filtering never shifts what a person is reading sideways.
