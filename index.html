Zane is a language for intent-driven user interfaces, held to a law

Zane is a declarative, compilable language for user interfaces: a declaration states what the user, a person or an agent, is doing, and a verifier refuses what the law forbids before anything is drawn.

Why Zane exists

Interfaces built with AI assistants repeat the same defects after explicit instruction. The author of Zane corrected 455 of them in 26 projects, 164 of them a defect corrected before, and decided to end the repetition from first principles.
