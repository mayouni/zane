The compilable language for user interfaces

Zui is a declarative language for intent-driven UIs. You declare what the human is trying to do — discover, select, confirm, undo — and the compiler refuses what its constitution forbids, before anything is rendered. Behind the language: a machine-verifiable law of 123 rules as data, v3.13. In front of it: one declaration for 10 media, and an audit that reads what an interface actually paints.

Ninety seconds of Zui

A payment approval, declared before a single pixel exists. Note what is absent: no widgets, no layout, no framework.

// payments.zui — design phase, day one
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW approve_payment (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT]      // interest → review → commitment
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check payments.zui
payments.zui:2  error  MISSING_UNDO  approve_payment
    An act declares no way back: SATISFIES holds ACT, ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks UNDO.
    Rule 79 · The Undo Covenant · Article IV · the right to reverse
payments.zui:2  error  MISSING_CANCEL  approve_payment
    A flow that acts cannot be abandoned: ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks CANCEL.
    Rule 26 · The Back Button is Holy · Article IV · the right to leave

2 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

The interface you were about to build has just been refused — at design time, by structure, citing the law by number and title. Declare the way back and it stands:

DEFINE INTENT_FLOW approve_payment (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, CANCEL]
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check payments.zui
clean — 1 file(s), 0 violations at level 2 against Zui v3.13

That is real output, copied from a real run, and this site's own check re-runs it: it fails if the page and the verifier ever disagree. The verifier ships in this repository and needs no toolchain beyond Node. The status board says plainly what exists, what is designed and what is held.

What is new: four things, each with its instrument

Each comes with the figure its instrument reports and the limit that figure has.

The language is declared

zui.stzu is StzZui written in Haro: 136 constraints, each citing the rule, article, right or pattern it enforces. A forge writes a second court and a grammar from it, and the two evaluators of the law (the verifier and the forged court) have 18 known differences, listed by seam. The judge fails on any other.

Limit: the forged court is silent on the warnings and on three checks that need facts a declaration does not hold.

One declaration, ten media

The ten media are enumerated in media.json, and a verb a medium cannot carry is refused. The coverage grid maps what a person does against them: 20 of 55 live cells have a rule written for that medium, 35 have none, and 89 of the 123 rules are written for a screen only.

Limit: the gap is the finding. Nobody has perceived any of these media for the grid. Media is being built.

The law reaches interfaces nobody declared

A screenshot is reduced to facts (text, box, luminances, contrast) and judged against the 3 rules a screenshot can decide. Every other rule reads not audited, never passed; a size is never a pass; contrast is judged at the worst end of the background.

Limit: no real application's screenshot has been run through it yet, and no person has looked at any. Audit

The refusal contract grew

37 diagnostics at 4 conformance levels, every finding carrying the rule it rests on, and 73 fixtures with known verdicts that any implementation can prove it agrees with. A rule was canonized from a check that had cited only an article.

Limit: level four holds no fixture by design: a painted layout is measured by the audit, not declared. The contract

What makes it different

It is a language, not a library. A human never comes to click a button; they come to discover, compare, act, confirm, undo. Zui makes that declarable — 10 declaration kinds over a closed grammar of 22 verbs — and therefore compilable: a selection that executes, a flow with no exit, an act with no undo are structural errors, refused by name before the interface exists.

Its semantics are a law, and the law is data. One versioned JSON file — 123 rules, each with its why, its evidence, its enforcement tier — is the single source of truth. The compiler checks against it, linters load it, findings cite it, and no prose can drift from what the machine enforces. Amendment requires documented evidence, never fashion.

It holds at three moments. At design time the declaration is checked before a pixel exists. In development every build is linted and every finding carries a rule number. At runtime the UI audit measures the painted page — sizes, true contrast, the path, the room, the menu against the tree.

What this site does not claim

  • No person has perceived any medium for the coverage grid; it is designed and measured, not verified by a person.
  • The screenshot audit has never been run on a real application.
  • The wider design, the Second Founding, is proposed, not ratified, and nothing here rests on it.
  • Held, not advanced: rules for haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal media until a product that ships in one is named; other pillars; a usability toolset.

Proof, not promises

This site tries to obey the law it publishes. Before each piece of this redesign shipped, its author ran the UI audit in both cultures and recorded the result with the commit; no gate enforces it yet. Press F2 to switch culture and notice that your rights don't flex (Article VI). What a violation feels like lets you commit each family of offence yourself, safely fenced, each beside its lawful twin, and the two-cultures exhibit renders one shared truth in both cultures at once.

The consumers, with their figures. The reference implementation is tools/zui, and this site's own check (tools/site-facts.js) re-runs it on 4 declarations shown on these pages and fails if the page and the verifier ever disagree. Zing, the first conforming consumer, pins law v3.11 while this site reads v3.13; a re-pin has been asked of it.

The stance is opinionated, openly: business applications first — operators, not explorers. Calm over power. Intent over mechanics. Visible over hidden. Undo is dignity.
