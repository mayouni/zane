The law of user interfaces

A machine-verifiable constitution for interfaces — 117 rules as data, derived from seven articles, each carrying its why and the real-world failure that earned it a number. Independent of any framework, any language, any platform. Opinionated toward business applications: operators, not explorers.

The problem

Interfaces are decaying in the AI era — and the generators are the amplifier, reproducing at machine speed every inherited assumption of the corpus they learned from. The evidence is recorded, not rhetorical: every rule numbered above 104 in this constitution was canonized from a defect an AI produced while building a real site — under explicit instruction to be careful. Small faint text. Verbless buttons. Controls floating over the reading. Paths that only look backwards. A menu contradicting the tree it claims to describe.

Without a law a machine can refuse to violate, the defects regenerate endlessly — and the discipline that once policed them has gone quiet.

The answer, in three layers

An intent grammar — 24 closed, medium-agnostic verbs (DISCOVER … EXIT). A human never comes to click a button; they come to discover, compare, act, confirm, undo. Widgets are consequences of intent, never primitives.

The constitution — one JSON file, the single source of truth. Linters load it, findings cite rule numbers, amendment requires evidence (never fashion), retired numbers are never reused.

Rendering projections — business web, CLI, voice, game worlds, and the AI-agent surface itself. A projection decides how to render; it has zero freedom over what: it cannot drop an UNDO, hide an available action, or strip EXIT from a flow.

Enter

The Reader — the whole law, interactive: search, keyboard navigation, enforcement badges, operator rights, forbidden patterns.

The Site Audit — measure six of the rules on any same-origin site, in your browser: computed size, true contrast, the full path, scrolling with room to spare, menu/tree coherence.

The harvest record — how eight rules were extracted from the ZinBank, Zin and Refine evidence trails. The architecture — the three layers and the consumer contract. The roadmap — from law-at-home to a standalone zui verifier to the educational showcase.

Who consumes it

StzWeb — the first conforming consumer: its linter cites rule numbers, its site scores zero violations under the audit. The Zin platform's Zui pillar shares the same articles and already compiles the intent grammar. Any project can join by pinning a version of the law and checking against it.
