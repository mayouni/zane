Language · for people who build a tool that reads StzZui

A claim to conform that your CI can refute.

A tool conforms when it gives the reference's verdict on every one of 73 fixtures, against a pack of 12 files pinned by hash. There is no library to adopt: agreement is proved by running the fixtures. This page shows the two commands, what they cannot say, and the status of every part of the system.

Two commands

The first asks whether the bytes you hold are the pack you meant. A version number says which edition you intended; the hash says the bytes are that edition. The second runs every fixture through the verifier and says which, if any, disagreed.

$ node tools/zui/pack.js --check

The Zui protocol pack
  law          v3.13 — 123 rules, 17 forbidden patterns
  language     v1.4 — 37 diagnostics
  axes         10 media, 5 semantic values
  proof        73 fixtures + the cross-medium spec
  pinned       12 files, sha256 over LF-normalised content
  README       every count on the page matches the manifest

  verified — every pinned file matches the manifest
$ node tools/zui/conformance.js

Zui conformance — level 4
  spec         doc/language/ v1.4
  contract     violations.json v1.8 (constitution v3.13)
  envelope     diagnostic-contract v1.2.0 — 56 diagnostics and 73 reports, all conform
  media        10 media — verifier agrees with media.json
  catalogue    37 diagnostics — contract agrees with VIOLATIONS.md
  fixtures     73 total
  pass 73 · fail 0 · skipped 0 (above level 4)

  conforms at level 4 — every fixture in the suite.

Both outputs above are re-run by this site's own check, so they cannot drift from the tools.

What "level 4" says here. The suite holds 12 fixtures at level one, 56 at level two, 5 at level three and 0 at level four. A claim at level four means every fixture there is passes, and the suite has none above level three, by design: a painted layout is the audit's to measure, not a declaration's. Read a level-four claim as a level-three claim.

What a pass does not prove

  • It proves agreement, not rightness. Two tools that refuse the same wrong thing pass together. The suite fixes what the law refuses; whether the law is right for a person is the question of perception, and no person has perceived this site's media grid.
  • It covers what the fixtures cover. 3 diagnostics have no fixture, on purpose, each with its reason in the manifest: one is a painted layout, two are about recovering from a malformed file, which the language does not define. A tool can pass the suite and differ on them.
  • A twin proves a tool does not over-refuse. The suite holds 22 lawful twins, files that differ from a refused one by one change. A tool that refuses everything fails them.
  • It does not make a port. A tool states its level, pins the manifest, emits the refusal envelope, and lists where it differs from the reference the way the forged court does.

What this site checks about itself

This site tries to obey the law it publishes. Before each piece of it shipped, its author ran the UI audit in both cultures and recorded the result with the commit; no gate enforces it yet. Press F2, or use the style select at the foot of any page, to switch culture and notice that your rights don't flex (Article VI). What a violation feels like lets you commit each family of offence yourself, safely fenced, each beside its lawful twin, and the two-cultures exhibit renders one shared truth in both cultures at once.

The consumers, with their figures. The reference implementation is tools/zui, and this site's own check (tools/site-facts.js) re-runs it on 7 declarations shown on these pages and fails if the page and the verifier ever disagree. Zing, the first conforming consumer, pins law v3.11 while this site reads v3.13; a re-pin has been asked of it.

What this site does not claim

  • No person has perceived any channel for the coverage grid; it is designed and measured, not verified by a person.
  • The screenshot audit has never been run on a real application.
  • The wider design, the Second Founding, is proposed, not ratified, and nothing here rests on it.
  • Held, not advanced: rules for haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal channels until a product that ships in one is named; other pillars; a usability toolset.

The stance is opinionated, openly: business applications first, operators and not explorers. Calm over power. Intent over mechanics. Visible over hidden. Undo is dignity.
