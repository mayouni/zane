Language · for people who build or judge interfaces

Say what the person is doing. The law refuses the rest, by name.

A declaration states the intent of a surface in a closed vocabulary of 22 verbs and 10 kinds of declaration, with no widget in it. This page walks the four steps from a declaration to a measured page, and says which of them exist today and where each one stops.

A guideline cannot fail a build

The practices you already use are good at what they do. They differ in when they speak and in whether anything can say no.

A published guideline

A widely used set of ten usability heuristics asks, among other things, that a person can always leave a state they did not mean to enter and take back what they did. It is applied by an expert reading a finished screen, so it finds problems only a person can see. It also cannot stop an unfinished screen from shipping: nothing in it refuses anything.

The same idea, as a law

// delete_invoice.zui
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW delete_invoice (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL, EXIT]
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check delete_invoice.zui
delete_invoice.zui:2  error  MISSING_UNDO  delete_invoice
    An act declares no way back: SATISFIES holds ACT, ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks UNDO.
    Rule 79 · The Undo Covenant · Article IV · the right to reverse

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

Both are right. The heuristic judges what only a person can judge; the law refuses what a machine can see before a pixel exists. This site does not claim the second replaces the first.

Four steps, and which of them exist

Declare. A .zui file says which intents a surface satisfies and what stays always reachable. There are 10 kinds of declaration and 22 verbs, closed on purpose: a widget is a consequence of an intent, never a primitive. The language is also declared as data, and a court can be made from that.

Refuse. The verifier reads the declaration against the law, which is data (123 rules, v3.13), and refuses what it forbids. Every refusal cites the rule, article or right it rests on. See one, line by line.

Project. One declaration can be rendered in more than one medium, and a verb a medium cannot carry is refused. That check is built; the media data is in conformance/media.json.

Measure. A declaration is not a page. The UI audit measures what a browser actually paints: size, true contrast, the bars, the room a block has, the menu against the tree.

The same law at three moments

What exists today, and what does not

  • Exists: the grammar and the verifier (37 diagnostics), the law as data, the UI audit, and a suite of 73 fixtures that any implementation can prove it agrees with.
  • Data, not renderers: the media carriage and the signal vocabulary. The pieces that draw, speak or print are not part of this repository.
  • Not built: computing the facts a declaration does not hold (the entities, actors, state and elements of a surface) from a host project. The verifier accepts a table you supply and says nothing without one.
  • Held, not advanced: rules for haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal media, until a product that ships in one is named.
