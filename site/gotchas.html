The experience · the argument, made playable

Feel what the law forbids

Every red frame below misbehaves on purpose — the way business software misbehaves every day. Trigger each offense yourself, feel the small violence of it, then do the same thing under the law. Each pair cites the rule that makes the difference enforceable. Offenses 8–12 are the 2026 harvest: each was committed by an AI, on a real application, before it was law.

1 · The Shove Rule 38 · Zero Displacement

You are reading. An error appears. Everything you were reading jumps.

The offense

The paragraph you were reading — watch it get shoved when the error barges in above it.

Under the law
We need an amount before saving — add one above.

The same paragraph. The error's space was reserved; when it arrives, nothing you were reading moves.

Why it is law: layout shifts break the ocular groove — the eye loses its place and the mind pays to find it again. Reserved space keeps the world still. The declaration checker names this violation LAYOUT_DISPLACEMENT.

2 · "Are you sure?" Rule 79 · The Undo Covenant

One version interrogates you before trusting you. The other trusts you first — and keeps a ten-second door open.

The offense
Invoice #204 — 45,000 XOF

A real dialog will interrupt you. Twice.

Under the law
Invoice #204 — 45,000 XOF

Why it is law: confirmation dialogs interrupt always and trust never; undo trusts first and interrupts never. Deletion happens instantly, visibly, and reversibly — dignity is a ten-second window. The checker's cousin, MISSING_UNDO, refuses flows that cannot say the way back.

3 · The Invisible Filter Rule 82 · Visible State

The boss asks: "why is revenue down?" It isn't. A filter someone left on is hiding half the rows.

The offense
Sales — August
Total

Looks complete, doesn't it?

Under the law
Sales — August filter: cash only ✕
Total

Click the chip to clear the filter — state is always visible, always dismissible.

Why it is law: a user who draws conclusions from state they cannot see has been harmed by the interface. The checker names it INVISIBLE_STATE; the same article obliges every norm to carry a human-readable message.

4 · The Div That Pretends Rule 80 · Keyboard Sovereignty

Two "buttons". Put your mouse away and try to reach them with Tab, then press Enter.

The offense
Approve payment

It's a styled <div onclick>. Tab skips straight past it — a keyboard operator simply cannot approve.

Under the law

A real <button>: focusable, visible focus ring, Enter and Space just work.

Why it is law: the keyboard is the operator's instrument; any critical act that requires the mouse demotes the expert — and excludes some users entirely. Conforming linters flag mouse-only clickables, outline:none, and positive tabindex.

5 · "Invalid input!" Rule 40 · Non-Accusatory Pathfinding

Type an incomplete email — say, aminata@ — in each and leave the field (Tab or click away).

The offense

Blames you, explains nothing, shouts in red.

Under the law

States the system's requirement, with a verb that fixes it. The error fades the moment you start correcting.

Why it is law: the system failed to understand — saying so, calmly, keeps dignity and momentum. Every error message must contain a verb that tells the user how to move.

6 · The Vanishing Label Rule 28 · Forms as Conversations

Start typing in both fields. Now pretend the phone rang and you came back two minutes later — what was the first field asking?

The offense

The placeholder was the label. You typed; it vanished; the question is gone.

Under the law

The label lives above the field, always visible — the conversation keeps its question on the table.

Why it is law: a form is the system asking; hiding the question mid-answer turns conversation into interrogation.

7 · The Lying Success Rule 18 · The Speed of Thought

Click both save buttons. One tells you what you want to hear. Watch what it does four seconds later.

The offense

"Success" before the system knew. Calm built on a lie collects its debt with interest.

Under the law

Acknowledged within 100ms — honestly: saving is not saved. Success is announced when it is true.

Why it is law: never show "Success" until the execution is confirmed — a fake progress bar is a lie, and trust is the only currency a business tool has.

Why it is law: a signal that appears everywhere is a signal that means nothing. Colour is spent in exactly one place — the column that answers "must I act?" — and every other column is plain text, however stateful its values. Born in ZinBank, April 2026, where green meant "healthy" in column 1 and "low drift" in column 8.

9 · The Card That Lies Rule 119 · A Card Is Not a Button

Three tiles, identically dressed. One navigates, two are information. Find out which — the only way this screen allows: poke them.

The offense
12 ›Active agents
Billing ›3 invoices due
low ›Drift
Under the law
12Active agents
lowDrift

Why it is law: form follows role, and each widget plays exactly one. A card is a read-only indicator; an action takes the shape of a button. When everything might be pressable, the operator must poke the interface to learn it — and an interface learned by poking was never read. Born in ZinBank: an autonomy ladder drawn as five clickable-looking cards.

10 · The Second Row Rule 120 · The Second Level Turns

Two rows of tabs, one over the other, dressed alike. Click around, then answer without reading twice: which row owns the page you are on?

The offense
…and which of the six is this page?
Under the law

Why it is law: when navigation nests, the second level changes axis — first level horizontal, second vertical. The turn of axis is the hierarchy, made visible without a single label. Orientation is carried by the geometry, not reconstructed from it. Born in ZinBank Studio, April 2026.

11 · The Masking Assistant Rule 121 · The Assistant Never Masks the Work

You are approving a payment. Both screens have an AI assistant. Open each one and watch what happens to your work.

The offense

How can I help?

I can explain this payment, check the beneficiary, or draft a note.

Even closed, its label sits over your Approve button.
Under the law

Assistant

Beside the work, never over it. Your form is still there.

A small mark, its name on approach.

Why it is law: the agent's calling surface is the one floating affordance an interface may carry, and it earns the exception by being minimal: a small circle, its name on hover, its panel never covering the work the operator is deciding about. A colleague at the elbow, not a billboard over the desk. Born in ZinBank, April 2026 — the first law written for the AI-agent surface itself.

12 · The Wall of Text Rule 123 · The Reading Arc

Two versions of the same product page. Scroll each to the bottom — then take the one-question quiz.

The offense

Our platform delivers a comprehensive, fully integrated solution for the modern enterprise, leveraging best-in-class architecture to enable seamless digital transformation across your organization's entire value chain, while our proprietary methodology ensures that every stakeholder touchpoint is optimized for maximal operational synergy, and furthermore the modular design of our components guarantees future-proof extensibility for requirements that have not yet been articulated by your teams, which is why industry analysts consistently recognize our approach as transformative, disruptive, and paradigm-shifting in equal measure.

In addition, our pricing model has been carefully engineered to align with the value realization curve of each deployment phase, beginning with an initial discovery engagement in which our consultants map your existing processes against our capability matrix, followed by a phased rollout calibrated to your organization's change-absorption capacity, and culminating in a continuous-improvement partnership whose quarterly business reviews ensure that the solution keeps pace with your evolving strategic priorities and regulatory obligations.

Our security posture, meanwhile, reflects a defense-in-depth philosophy encompassing encryption at rest and in transit, role-based access control with fine-grained permission scoping, comprehensive audit logging, and a shared-responsibility model documented across more than forty pages of compliance collateral available upon request from your account representative, who will also be pleased to walk you through our certification portfolio and our incident-response commitments.

Finally, our customer success organization stands ready to accompany you at every step of the journey, from onboarding through adoption to expansion, with a tiered support offering whose response-time guarantees are among the most competitive in the industry, complemented by a vibrant community forum, a certification academy, and an annual user conference at which our product roadmap is unveiled to our valued ecosystem of partners and practitioners.

Under the law

What it is

One platform for your operations. How it works →

What it costs

Three phases, priced per phase — discovery, rollout, partnership.

How it is secured

Encrypted, role-scoped, audit-logged. The full posture is one document, linked, not pasted.

Who helps you

A named team from onboarding onward. Support tiers on one page.

Where to go deeper

Each section above links out. Depth is below the fold, not in your way.

Why it is law: a reading surface has a budget — roughly ten to twelve sections, general to specific, skimmable value on top, depth below, links out for the rest. A paragraph past eight hundred characters unbroken has become a wall: a lead plus a link. A reader who has met twenty sections remembers none of them. Born in the Refine rebuild, June 2026.
