The experience · the argument, made playable

All families of violations on these pages are behaviours of an interface that a rule of the Zane Constitution forbids, grouped by the rules they break.

In all seven families, a violation on these pages is a red frame that misbehaves on purpose, in the way business software misbehaves every day, so that a person can trigger the offense and feel it. The pages let a person feel what each rule prevents before reading the rule. The twelve violations are grouped by rule family, and each one sits beside its lawful twin, which does the same thing under the law. Each pair cites the rule that makes the difference enforceable. An AI committed five of the twelve violations on a real application before they became law.

The rule families and the violations in each family

A family is a jurisdiction of the constitution, and it grows as the harvest of rules grows. Each new rule whose offense a person can feel receives its duel on these pages.

Stillness

The Shove · 38 — The Lying Success · 18

The rules of stillness keep the frame still, and they forbid the interface to say "done" before it is true.

Forms

"Invalid input!" · 40 — The Vanishing Label · 28

The rules of forms treat a form as a conversation: the question stays visible, and the system never blames the user, a person or an agent, for its own confusion.
