Feel what the law forbids

Twelve offenses, organized by rule family. Every red frame misbehaves on purpose — the way business software misbehaves every day. Trigger each offense yourself, feel the small violence of it, then do the same thing under the law. Each pair cites the rule that makes the difference enforceable — and five of the twelve were committed by an AI, on a real application, before they were law.
