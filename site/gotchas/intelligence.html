Gotchas · the intelligence family — born in the 2026 harvest

Intelligence is the family of rules for the AI-agent surface, which keeps the agent beside a person's work and never over it.

Intelligence is the newest jurisdiction of the law, and its rules govern the AI-agent surface itself, the place where an AI agent offers help inside an interface. The rules ensure that the agent acts like a colleague at the person's elbow and never covers the work like a billboard over the desk. This family holds one violation today, and it grows with the AI-interaction jurisdiction (M5): the déluge of the chat stream, the consent surface, and the generator that ships scaffolding as a deliverable (Rule 124) all have duels planned.

Assistant

Beside the work, never over it. Your form is still there.

A small mark, its name on approach.

Why it is law: the surface that calls the agent is the one floating affordance an interface may carry, and it earns that exception by being minimal: it is a small circle that shows its name on hover, and its panel never covers the work the operator is deciding about. The agent is a colleague at the elbow, not a billboard over the desk. The rule comes from the banking product in April 2026, and it is the first law written for the AI-agent surface itself.
