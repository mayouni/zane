What the eye is owed

Scanning a table and reading a page are the same act at two speeds, and both run on a budget the interface must respect. The two offenses here overspend it: a table where one colour means two things, and a page that spends your memory faster than it earns your attention. Both were committed by an AI on a real application before they were law.
