The thinking behind an intent-driven interface

This section holds the reasoning, the grid of what an interface must let a person do, and the comparison with what already exists. The essay is being written; what exists today is the documents it will draw on.

The design documents

The reasoning is written in the repository. In the order a reader would take them: the founding architecture (intent before widgets, three layers); the interaction model (one declaration, many renderings, one law); sensors and actuators (what the closed vocabulary survives); stzSense (how style is declared); a GUI system, read by the law; the coverage map (where the law speaks and where it is silent).

One more is proposed and not ratified: the Second Founding, the wider design. Nothing on this site rests on it.

What is already on the site: the comparison with the practices a reader already uses.

What this site does not show yet

The essay, the coverage grid as a page, and the claims with their limits are not written. The grid is designed and measured and not verified by a person.
