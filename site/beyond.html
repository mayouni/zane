Use · for conversational, voice and UI-less applications

Zane checks interfaces with no screen, such as a voice line, a chime, a sign or an agent, against what a user, a person or an agent, must be able to do there.

This page applies Zane to interfaces with no screen, such as a phone line, a status chime, an agent that a machine drives and a sign in a lobby, which still promise that a user can leave, can undo and can tell what state the interface is in. The verifier ensures that a declaration for such an interface promises only what its channel can carry, and keeps the way out and the state within reach. The page runs four declarations for real and says what each channel decides.

A voice line that promises a verb a voice cannot carry

A person who asks for a balance over the phone, in a flow that promises ZOOM, is promised something a voice cannot carry. The verifier refuses the promise, and it does not refuse the voice line:

DEFINE VIBE phone_line (
  SURFACE_AS voice,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW check_balance (
  SATISFIES [UNDERSTAND, ZOOM],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL, EXIT],
  ACTOR caller
)
$ zane check check_balance.zane
check_balance.zane:5  error  VERB_NOT_CARRIED  ZOOM in check_balance on voice
    A declaration satisfies, or promises to keep reachable, an intent the declared medium cannot carry at all.
    Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article II

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

An agent surface that acts and offers no way to cancel

An interface for an agent is an interface: the agent is the user at the other end, and the law about leaving and undoing applies to it as much as to anyone. The verifier refuses a flow that places orders and cannot be abandoned:

DEFINE VIBE agent_api (
  SURFACE_AS machine,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW place_order (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, EXIT],
  ACTOR agent
)
$ zane check agent_order.zane
agent_order.zane:5  error  MISSING_CANCEL  place_order
    A flow that acts cannot be abandoned: ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks CANCEL.
    Rule 26 · The Back Button is Holy · Article IV · the right to leave

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

The whole fix is to declare the way out:

DEFINE VIBE agent_api (
  SURFACE_AS machine,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW place_order (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, CANCEL, EXIT],
  ACTOR agent
)
$ zane check agent_order_fixed.zane
clean — 1 file(s), 0 violations at level 2 against Zane v3.16

Two rules are written for this channel, and they are judgment rules, so the verifier does not check them: Rule 121, the assistant never masks the work, and Rule 124, the generator ships a working thing. They are the only rules the law has for agent surfaces, and the last section of this page shows, from the grid, why that coverage is thin.

A status chime whose every status renders as silence

A surface may declare a status that a channel renders as silence, and silence is the correct rendering of "waiting, inactive". The defect is narrower: when every status that a surface declares renders as nothing in the channel it declared, the surface promises a perceptible state and delivers none:

DEFINE VIBE chime_only (
  SURFACE_AS auditory,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTERACTION job_status (
  SATISFIES [UNDERSTAND],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [EXIT],
  SIGNALS [muted],
  ROLE inert,
  ACTOR operator
)
$ zane check chime.zane
chime.zane:8  error  SIGNAL_UNPERCEIVABLE  muted in job_status on auditory
    Every semantic status a surface declares renders as absence in the medium it declared.
    Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article III · the right to know

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

This refusal enforces the right to know for a channel with no screen. Only two channels have a recorded rendering of statuses today (sound and voice). In any other channel the verifier is silent, and its silence means undecided, not fine.

A sign that promises to act

A fixed sign carries wayfinding and almost nothing else. A declaration for a lobby screen that promises ACT on a sign draws four refusals, which concern the verbs a sign cannot carry (ACT and EXIT) and the undo and cancel that an act owes. The suite's own printed-ledger case draws the same kind of refusals for a page that promises an act. The fixed channels, such as print, signage and a lamp, are the channels that cannot carry most verbs.

Channels beyond the screen where the law has no rule

On the grid, 5 of the ten channels have no rule written for them at all (haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal), and the voice, sound and machine channels have a rule on only a few cells. The grid draws this coverage and leaves the empty cells empty. The refusals above are real, and they are the checks that exist: a verb a channel cannot carry, a flow that cannot be left, and a status that renders as nothing. They are not a full account of what a good voice line or a good agent surface is.

The verifier checks the declaration, and a person judges whether a voice line is heard as intended and whether an agent surface serves its agent.
