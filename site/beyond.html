Use · for conversational, voice and UI-less applications

When there is nothing to look at, the law still says what the person must be able to do.

A phone line, a status chime, an agent that a machine drives and a sign in a lobby have no screen, and still make promises: you can leave, you can undo, you can tell what state it is in. Four declarations, run for real, and what each channel decides.

On this page

A voice line cannot zoom

A person asking for their balance over the phone, in a flow that promises ZOOM, has been promised something a voice cannot carry. The verifier refuses the promise, not the voice line:

DEFINE VIBE phone_line (
  SURFACE_AS voice,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW check_balance (
  SATISFIES [UNDERSTAND, ZOOM],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [CANCEL, EXIT],
  ACTOR caller
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check check_balance.zui
check_balance.zui:5  error  VERB_NOT_CARRIED  ZOOM in check_balance on voice
    A declaration satisfies, or promises to keep reachable, an intent the declared medium cannot carry at all.
    Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article II

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

An agent can act and cannot leave

An interface for an agent is an interface: the agent is the person at the other end, and the law about leaving and undoing applies to it as much as to anyone. A flow that places orders and cannot be abandoned is refused:

DEFINE VIBE agent_api (
  SURFACE_AS machine,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW place_order (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, EXIT],
  ACTOR agent
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check agent_order.zui
agent_order.zui:5  error  MISSING_CANCEL  place_order
    A flow that acts cannot be abandoned: ALWAYS_REACHABLE lacks CANCEL.
    Rule 26 · The Back Button is Holy · Article IV · the right to leave

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

Declaring the way out is the whole fix:

DEFINE VIBE agent_api (
  SURFACE_AS machine,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTENT_FLOW place_order (
  SATISFIES [SELECT, CONFIRM, ACT],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [UNDO, CANCEL, EXIT],
  ACTOR agent
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check agent_order_fixed.zui
clean — 1 file(s), 0 violations at level 2 against Zui v3.13

Two rules are written for this channel and are judgment rules, so the verifier does not check them: Rule 121, the assistant never masks the work, and Rule 124, the generator ships a working thing. They are the only rules the law has for agent surfaces, and the grid below says why that is thin.

A chime that says "nothing" says nothing

A surface may declare a status that a channel renders as silence, and silence is the correct rendering of "waiting, inactive". The defect is narrower: when every status a surface declares is rendered as nothing in the channel it declared, the surface promised a perceptible state and delivered none:

DEFINE VIBE chime_only (
  SURFACE_AS auditory,
  MOTION none
)
DEFINE INTERACTION job_status (
  SATISFIES [UNDERSTAND],
  ALWAYS_REACHABLE [EXIT],
  SIGNALS [muted],
  ROLE inert,
  ACTOR operator
)
$ node tools/zui/cli.js check chime.zui
chime.zui:8  error  SIGNAL_UNPERCEIVABLE  muted in job_status on auditory
    Every semantic status a surface declares renders as absence in the medium it declared.
    Rule 82 · Perceivable State · Article III · the right to know

1 error(s), 0 warning(s) across 1 file(s) at level 2

That is the right to know, enforced for a channel with no screen. Only two channels have a recorded rendering of statuses today (sound and voice); in any other the verifier is silent, which means undecided, not fine.

A sign that also acts

A fixed sign carries wayfinding, and almost nothing else. A declaration for a lobby screen that promises ACT on a sign draws four refusals: the verbs a sign cannot carry (ACT and EXIT) and the undo and cancel that an act owes. The suite's own printed-ledger case draws the same kind of refusals for a page that promises an act. The channels that cannot carry most verbs are the fixed ones: print, signage, a lamp.

Where the law is silent

For these channels the grid has 5 of ten with no rule written for them at all (haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal), and the voice, sound and machine channels have a rule on only a few cells. The grid draws it, empty cells empty. The refusals above are real, and they are the checks that exist: a verb a channel cannot carry, a flow that cannot be left, a status that renders as nothing. They are not a full account of what a good voice line or a good agent surface is.

Limit: the verifier checks the declaration. Whether a voice line is heard as intended, or an agent surface is usable by the agent, has no instrument here, and nobody has perceived any of these channels.
