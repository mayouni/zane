Use · for people who implement Zane in another language

Zane can be implemented in other languages by writing its verifier and running its fixtures, not by adopting a library.

Porting Zane to other languages means writing a verifier that is a peer of the one in this repository: it reads the same pinned files, parses the same grammar, and reports the same diagnostics on the same fixtures. The fixture suite ensures that a port proves its agreement instead of asserting it, and the suite names any fixture on which the port disagrees. The page gives the recipe and the limits of what a pass lets you say.

The recipe for implementing Zane in another language

  1. Vendor the pack and pin its hash. A version number says which edition you meant, and the hash proves that the bytes you hold are that edition. tools/zane/pack.js --check
  2. Implement the grammar from doc/language/GRAMMAR.md: the ten kinds, the closed enumerations and the 22 verbs.
  3. Run the 73 fixtures at the level you claim. Compare by diagnostic id only, never by line number, wording or order. A fixture above your level is skipped and recorded, never failed.
  4. Emit the refusal envelope (the six fields of What it refuses) so that one CI step can read any court.
  5. List your differences from the reference, as the twin ratchet does: each difference is a named row, and any other difference is a failure. Declared in Haro shows one such list.

What a port may claim, and what it may not claim

A port may claim "conforms to Zane v3.14 at level 2, verified against manifest <hash>", and it may use its own diagnostic codes in its own words and its own language for the prose.

A port may not extend a closed set. The verbs, the channels, the semantic values, the enumerations and the ten kinds of declaration are closed. Adding to one is a language change or a constitutional amendment, and an implementation that adds a verb is a different language that fails the fixtures.

Three claims are separate: conformance (the fixtures agree), envelope conformance (your findings travel in the family's shape), and cross-channel conformance. The last claim does not fully pass today, in this repository either: Channels shows its real output, which is proved for one pair and not proved for another.

Who has implemented Zane so far

The reference is the JavaScript verifier in this repository. A verifier in Zig, with a browser build, is designed. Its first stage, the check, is built, and it prints what the JavaScript verifier prints, byte for byte, on every fixture and on a generated corpus (tools/zane/parity.js). The Zig check has run on Windows only, and the browser build is not built.

Limit: no implementation outside this family has been written. The recipe is a procedure for a stranger, and it is not a record of a stranger succeeding.
