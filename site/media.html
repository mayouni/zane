Channels · being built

One declaration, ten channels

This section is being built. What exists today is data and instruments in the repository; this page names them and says what the site does not yet show. A channel is what the language and the law call a medium.

What exists today

The ten channels a declaration can name, and what each carries, are published as data in conformance/media.json (the keyword in a declaration is SURFACE_AS, and the diagnostics say medium). A verb a channel cannot carry is refused by the verifier, and the signal vocabulary and the statuses that render as silence are in conformance/signal.json.

The instrument that checks that renderings differ only as that data predicts is tools/zui/cross-medium.js, and the map of where the law has something to say, and where it is silent, is doc/design/COVERAGE.md in the repository.

What this site does not show yet

The pages that present the channels, the coverage grid and its gaps, and the cross-channel proof are not written. Nobody has perceived any of these channels for this grid, so the grid is designed and measured and not verified by a person.
