Which way in is yours?

Seven kinds of reader, each with a first page that exists. Pick the one that sounds like your next week; every way in rests on the same law, the same refusal contract and the same proof.

Seven ways in

You build business software

Declare intent before you render, gate your CI on the law, measure the painted page in your browser. Framework-independent by construction: the declaration outlives your stack.

Build →

You design, or you care about UX

The full argument: what is decaying, why guidelines failed, and the recorded evidence, defect by defect.

Read the case →

You build with AI

Make the generator declare intent before it draws, and see what a grammar can hold and what only the court can. Say honestly what has not been measured.

Generate →

You ship where there is no screen

Voice, sound, print, an API a machine reads: one declaration, ten media, and an honest account of which cells have a rule written for them. This section is being built.

Channels →

You must judge an application you do not own

The audit measures what a page paints; a second audit, designed and never yet run on a real application, reads a screenshot and says plainly which rules it can decide and which it refuses to.

Audit →

You design a language

StzZui is itself declared, in Haro. The declaration is the worked example: constraints that each cite the rule they enforce, and a forged court and grammar that come from it.

You implement StzZui elsewhere

Pin the pack, run the suite, and keep two evaluators honest with a list of their known differences. Zing is the first conforming consumer.

What every way in shares

One law, as data: 123 rules in one versioned file, v3.13. One refusal contract: 37 diagnostics, each bound to the rule, article or right it enforces. One proof: 73 fixtures with known verdicts, so an implementation can prove it agrees instead of asserting it. None of it needs more than Node and a browser.

Where nothing is written yet

Stated as held or designed, so that no way in promises a page that is not there.

  • The media pages are being built. What exists today is data and instruments in the repository.
  • The screenshot audit has a written fact schema and a writer; no real application's screenshot has been run through it, and no person has looked at one.
  • Rules for haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal channels are held until a product that ships in one is named.
  • The wider design, the Second Founding, is proposed, not ratified. This page does not rest on it.
