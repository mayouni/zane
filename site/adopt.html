The reasons, and the case against the practices you already use, are in Comparison and Why a law. What is built, designed and held, part by part, is the status board.

What every way in shares

  • One law, as data: 123 rules in one versioned file, v3.14.
  • One refusal contract: 37 diagnostics, each bound to the rule, article or right it enforces.
  • One proof: 73 fixtures with known verdicts, so an implementation can prove it agrees instead of asserting it.
  • Today it needs Node and a browser, and nothing else. A single file that needs neither is designed and not built.

Where nothing is written yet

  • The screenshot audit has a written fact schema and a checker; no real application's screenshot has been run through it, and no person has looked at one.
  • Rules for haptic, luminous, signage, spatial and terminal channels are held until a product that ships in one is named.
  • The wider design, the Second Founding, is proposed, not ratified. Nothing on this page rests on it.
