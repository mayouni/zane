Use · for people who build inside Softanza

Inside Softanza the law is a file today, and three pieces are held.

Softanza is the family of projects this belongs to. What adopting the law there means now is small and real; what it would mean next is named as held, with where each piece would live, and nothing is asked of any other repository from this page.

Today

  • The law and the pack are files a Softanza project can vendor: 12 files and a manifest, checked by node tools/zane/pack.js --check.
  • The UI audit runs on any page a project renders, as the web case shows on the Softanza site's own navigation, read only.
  • The Softanza site is itself written under the law. Its Zui page says which rules it applies. It reports, as read on 1 October 2026, law 3.11 and fifty fixtures; this repository is at v3.13 and 73, so that page is behind, and says when it was read.
  • Zing, a separate open-source framework in the family, is the first tool that consumes the law, and pins v3.11. A re-pin has been asked of it.

Held, with where each piece would live

Three pieces are not built, and none has been started. They are recorded here from the design record and have not been re-checked against Softanza's libraries by this page.

  • A reader for .zane files inside Softanza's own library: today the verifier is outside it.
  • A projection from a declaration to a Softanza surface: a declaration is checked, and not yet turned into anything Softanza draws.
  • A court hosted inside Softanza: the court forged from the Haro declaration runs through a checkout of the Ring++ spike (Declared in Haro); a court that lives in Softanza itself does not exist.

Limit: nobody on the Softanza library side has been asked whether any of the three is wanted. The page will say "asked" when that has happened, and until then says it has not.
