Use · for people who build inside Softanza

Softanza is the family of projects that Zane belongs to, and inside Softanza the law is a set of files today, with three further pieces held.

Softanza is the family of projects that Zane belongs to, and adopting the law inside Softanza means vendoring the law and its pack as files and running the UI audit on any page a project renders. Vendoring the law lets a Softanza project check its declarations against a pinned edition of the law. The page names three further pieces as held, says where each piece would live, and asks nothing of any other repository.

What adopting the law inside Softanza means today

  • The law and the pack are files that a Softanza project can vendor: 12 files and a manifest, which node tools/zane/pack.js --check checks.
  • The UI audit runs on any page a project renders, as the web case shows on the Softanza site's own navigation, which it only reads.
  • The Softanza site is itself written under the law. Its Zui page says which rules it applies. That page reports the figures it read on 1 October 2026: law 3.11 and fifty fixtures. This repository is at v3.14 and 73 fixtures, so that page is behind, and it says when it was read.
  • Zing, a separate open-source framework in the family, is the first tool that consumes the law, and it pins v3.11. A re-pin has been asked of Zing.

Held, with where each piece would live

Three pieces are not built, and none has been started. This page records them from the design record and has not re-checked them against Softanza's libraries.

  • A reader for .zane files inside Softanza's own library. Today the verifier is outside that library.
  • A projection from a declaration to a Softanza surface. A declaration is checked today, and it is not yet turned into anything that Softanza draws.
  • A court hosted inside Softanza. The court forged from the Haro declaration runs through a checkout of the Ring++ spike (Declared in Haro), and no court lives in Softanza itself.

Limit: nobody on the Softanza library side has been asked whether any of the three pieces is wanted. The page will say "asked" when that has happened, and until then it says that nobody has been asked.
