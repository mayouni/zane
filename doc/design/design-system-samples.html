Zane · the design system (ZANE-DESIGN-01)

Each principle of Zane's design system is shown here as a live sample, the lawful one beside the offending one.

This page holds thirteen pairs of real interface samples, one pair for each principle of the design system. Each caption cites the rule the lawful sample follows and the corrections in the corpus that the offending one repeats.

Every text is set for reading

Reading text is 21 px, secondary text is never under 18 px, and every text stands at 7:1 against what is under it.

Width belongs to the content

A box is as wide as what it holds, and its control sits beside the words it acts on; the width left over stays empty.

A line or a box draws something real

Space groups the points of a list; a border appears only where it draws a table, a field, a drawing or a window.

One emphasis per element, one critical element per view

A link is told by one cue, and a page singles out one thing that is wrong.

Balance is on by default

Each sentence of a title is balanced on its own, and no line carries the end of one sentence into the next.

One shape, one meaning

A two-state control shows both states, and a control's word says what it does to the item.

The menu is the tree

A section's landing page comes first in its band, under a name of its own.

Everything is on the page, with one scroll

What a control does is written in the page, and a list grows with the page instead of scrolling inside it.

Every input reaches everything

A target is 48 px tall on a phone, and nothing is reachable only by hovering.

Words serve the reader

A title names its subject in a full declarative sentence.

The page speaks for its reader

A public page states what the project is and does, at its true size.

The painted page is the instrument

A label clears the line it names, which only a look at the drawn result can confirm.

Decided stays decided

A checkbox takes its culture's colour; no state of a control is left to the platform.
