# The Harvest — August 2026

How Rules 117–124 entered the law. Every rule in the Zui Constitution requires real-world evidence (Article VII); this document records where each of the eight came from, so the evidence trail survives the projects that produced it.

## Sources

Three bodies of recorded experience, all from building real interfaces with AI assistance in 2026:

1. **ZinBank** (April 2026) — a full agentic banking platform UI, built and corrected screen by screen. The corrections were recorded as reusable principles at the time.
2. **Zin i18n decisions** (May 2026) — the language architecture adopted for ZinFoundry, the DRMs, and all Zin products.
3. **Refine site rebuild** (June 2026) — a ground-up redesign of a product site after the author judged the first version unreadable and unnavigable.

A fourth source frames the whole effort: the **StzWeb site corrections** (August 2026), where Rules 105–116 were each canonized from a defect the AI produced live. The pattern across all four is identical — capable generators reproducing inherited conventions until a written, checkable law stops them.

## The eight rules

| Rule | Title | Set | Source defect |
|---|---|---|---|
| 117 | One Column Carries the Colour | 2 · Visual Principles | ZinBank supervision tables: green meant "agent healthy" in column 1 and "low drift" in column 8 |
| 118 | Two Families of Colour | 2 · Visual Principles | ZinBank: one-colour-per-agent and per-channel palettes grew without bound and signified nothing |
| 119 | A Card Is Not a Button | 13 · Widget Selection | ZinBank: the L0–L4 ladder drawn as five cards instead of a table; card-styled clickable tiles |
| 120 | The Second Level Turns | 9 · Menu Ergonomics | ZinBank Studio: two stacked horizontal tab rows, indistinguishable hierarchy |
| 121 | The Assistant Never Masks the Work | 31 · Intelligence Under Law | ZinBank: floating agent with a permanent label masking the interface |
| 122 | Language Is Data | 18 · Multilingualism & Dialects | Zin: languages added by forking templates; French UIs full of untranslated jargon |
| 123 | The Reading Arc | 3 · Layout & IA | Refine: a 22-subsection homepage the author judged "text-heavy, dense, poor for onboarding" |
| 124 | The Generator Ships a Working Thing | 31 · Intelligence Under Law | Zin build-target culture: scaffolding is not a deliverable — every target opens working, with data and its own guide |

## What was deliberately not harvested

- **"Studio" over "Atelier des agents"** and other vocabulary picks — product naming, not law.
- **Navigation restructurings specific to ZinBank** (compositions inside the Studio, DSI's test mode) — instances of Rule 116 (the menu tells the truth about the tree), already law.
- **Refine's small+faint text findings** — independently canonized as Rules 105 and 107 before this harvest; the convergence from two unrelated projects is itself evidence that those rules carry weight.

## Method note

The harvest was performed by reading the projects' recorded feedback memories, not by re-auditing the codebases. What qualifies a principle for canonization: it must name a defect that (a) actually occurred, (b) was actually corrected, (c) generalizes beyond its project, and (d) is decidable — a reviewer can say yes or no against it. Principles that are taste, naming, or product strategy stay in their projects.
